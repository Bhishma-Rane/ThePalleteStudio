About

Hi, I'm Priti Rane

PR

With years of hands-on teaching experience, I founded The Palette Studio to give every student — from curious five-year-olds to lifelong beginners — a space to explore colour, technique, and confidence at their own pace.

My classes blend gentle structure with plenty of room for imagination, so every canvas feels personal. I believe art is best learned in small circles, not crowded classrooms — which is why every batch is capped at just two to three students.
